Privacy Policy

Sage Stone, legally SageStone Inc. ("SageStone," "we," "us," or "our"), provides structured remote support services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, protect, and retain information submitted through sagestoneinc.com, our contact forms, calls, email, and SMS/text messaging.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, work email address, company name, mobile or business phone number, service interest, message details, and SMS consent status. We may also collect communications records, scheduling details, and ordinary website analytics such as browser type, pages viewed, and referring page.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, schedule discovery calls, provide services, send operational updates, manage customer support, maintain consent and opt-out records, improve our website, protect our systems, and comply with legal obligations.

SMS/text messaging privacy

Sage Stone uses SMS/MMS messaging only for people who consent to receive texts about inquiries, scheduling, service updates, customer support, and active engagements. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer mobile phone numbers or SMS consent data to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. See our SMS Terms & Conditions for complete text messaging terms.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate our website, email, messaging, scheduling, analytics, accounting, and client-service systems; with SageStone personnel assigned to support your account; when required by law; or as part of a business transfer subject to this Policy.

Cookies and analytics

We use limited cookies and analytics to understand site performance and improve the visitor experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data security and retention

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we hold, including HTTPS, access controls, and confidentiality obligations. We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, to maintain consent and opt-out records, or to meet legal and business obligations.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information by contacting us. You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request help by replying HELP or contacting us directly.
