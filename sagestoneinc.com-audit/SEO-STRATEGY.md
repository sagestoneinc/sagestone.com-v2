# SageStone Inc — SEO Strategy & Keyword Ranking Plan

**Goal:** Rank for commercial "hire a VA / outsource support / Philippines staffing" intent and generate qualified discovery calls.
**Reality check:** Nothing can rank until the technical blockers (noindex, CSR, missing meta) are fixed. The roadmap below is ordered so you unblock first, then compete.

---

## 1. Positioning → Keyword Universe

SageStone sits in a **high-volume, high-competition** niche (VA / BPO / offshore staffing). Winning strategy = **don't fight the giants head-on for "virtual assistant"** at first. Win on:
- **Service + Philippines modifiers** (medium competition, high intent)
- **Audience/industry long-tails** (agencies, e-commerce, real estate, founders)
- **Bottom-funnel commercial terms** ("hire", "outsource", "services", "company")
- **Informational blog content** to build topical authority and feed internal links.

### Keyword clusters

**Primary cluster (commercial, core money terms)**
- virtual assistant services · hire a virtual assistant · outsourced customer support · back office outsourcing · executive assistant services · remote operations support

**Secondary cluster (Philippines / offshore modifier — SageStone's edge)**
- virtual assistant Philippines · hire Filipino virtual assistant · outsourcing to the Philippines · offshore staffing Philippines · Philippines customer support outsourcing · remote staff Philippines

**Audience / industry long-tail (easiest early wins)**
- virtual assistant for real estate agents · customer support for e-commerce brands · back office support for agencies · executive assistant for founders · operations support for small business

**Informational / blog (topical authority, top-funnel)**
- how much does a virtual assistant cost · in-house vs outsourced customer support · how to hire a virtual assistant · benefits of hiring in the Philippines · what does a back office team do · VA vs executive assistant

**Branded**
- sagestone · sagestone inc · sagestone virtual assistant

## 3. Internal Linking Plan (hub-and-spoke)

- **Services hub** (`/services`) links down to all 6 service detail pages; each service links back up + sideways to 1–2 related services.
- Each **service page** links to `/why-philippines` (proof of talent quality) + a relevant **industry** + a relevant **case study** + `/contact`.
- **Industries** page links each industry blurb to the services most relevant to it.
- **Blog posts** link to the money page they support (e.g. "How much does a VA cost" → `/services/virtual-assistant`).
- Add contextual body links, not just nav — Google weights in-content links higher.

## 5. Prioritized Ranking Roadmap (easiest wins first)

### Phase 0 — UNBLOCK (Week 1) 🔴 *do these before anything else*
1. **Remove `noindex, nofollow`** from `index.html`.
2. Add **`robots.txt`** (`Allow: /`, reference sitemap) and a static **`sitemap.xml`** with all routes.
3. Fix the **homepage `<title>` + meta description** to match the business (see table).
4. Fix **placeholder contact info** — real email on `sagestoneinc.com`, real phone, real/serviceable address; make Privacy/Terms links live.
5. Fix the **`*` catch-all** to render a real 404 (with proper status) instead of Home.

### Phase 1 — MAKE IT CRAWLABLE & PER-PAGE (Weeks 2–3) 🟠
6. Add **per-page metadata** — install `react-helmet-async` and set unique title/description/canonical/OG per route, OR (better) migrate to **SSG/SSR** (Vite SSG, or move to Next.js/Astro) so crawlers and AI bots get real HTML. *Prerendering is the single highest-leverage upgrade after removing noindex.*
7. Add **Open Graph + Twitter card** tags for share previews.
8. Add **JSON-LD**: `Organization` + `WebSite` site-wide; `BreadcrumbList` on inner pages.

### Phase 2 — ON-PAGE OPTIMIZATION (Weeks 3–4) 🟡
9. Rewrite **H1s and body copy** on each page around its primary keyword (table above), keeping the premium tone.
10. Add **`Service` schema** to each service page and **`FAQPage` schema** to `/faq` + service FAQs.
11. Self-host & optimize **images** (WebP/AVIF, explicit dimensions, lazy-load, real alt text).
12. Build the **internal linking** structure (Section 3).

### Phase 3 — AUTHORITY & CONTENT (Month 2+) 🟢
13. Replace **fabricated testimonials/case studies/stats** with real proof (or clearly-labeled illustrative examples). This is both a trust and a compliance issue.
14. Launch the **`/blog`** with the first 3–4 posts from the roadmap.
15. Set up **Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster + GA4**; submit sitemap; verify indexation.
16. Create/claim **Google Business Profile** and consistent **social profiles**; wire them into `Organization.sameAs`.
17. Begin **link building**: directory listings (Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush for BPO/staffing), guest posts, partner backlinks.

### Phase 4 — MONITOR & ITERATE (Ongoing) 🔵
18. Track rankings for the mapped keywords; watch GSC impressions/clicks/CTR by page.
19. Expand blog cadence (2–4 posts/month) targeting long-tails.
20. Add `Review`/`AggregateRating` schema once real reviews exist. Refresh meta by CTR.

## 6. What to Expect
- **Weeks 1–2:** Removing noindex + sitemap → Google begins indexing (branded + long-tail terms appear first).
- **Month 1–2:** With prerendering + per-page meta + schema, service/Philippines long-tails start ranking.
- **Month 3–6:** Blog + internal links + backlinks build authority; competitive commercial terms climb.
- Offshore-staffing head terms ("virtual assistant") are very competitive; realistic early wins are the **Philippines-modified** and **industry long-tail** terms, plus **branded** search.
